 Usage: words.py letter-freq [OPTIONS] {filename}                               
                                                                                
 Generate the frequency distribution for the total of all letters in all the    
 words across all years in the unigram.

╭─ Options ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────╮
│ --output  -o        display letter frequencies to standard output            │
